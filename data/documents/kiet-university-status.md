# KIET University Status and Accreditations

**Source:** https://www.kiet.edu/about/Overview/ and https://www.kiet.edu/ranking-accreditations/ugc/ (fetched 11 November 2026)
**Regime:** Institute-wide — applies to all batches.
**Fetched:** 11 November 2026

## Deemed-to-be University status

KIET was granted Autonomous Status in 2024 and subsequently conferred Deemed to be University status under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956, in November 2025. The official name in the Gazette notification is Krishna Institute of Engineering & Technology (KIET), Ghaziabad. KIET brands itself "KIET Deemed to be University" / "KIET University". The official website remains https://www.kiet.edu/ — no new domain.

## About the institute

KIET was established in 1998 by the Krishna Charitable Society. It has 10,000+ students across Engineering, Computer Applications, Management, Pharmacy and allied disciplines, and a global alumni network of 26,000+ professionals. It holds NAAC A+ accreditation and NBA accreditation for all eligible programmes, with recognition in NIRF and IIRF rankings and a QS I-Gauge Diamond Rating. The campus is on Ghaziabad–Meerut Road, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh – 201206, in the Delhi-NCR region, about 4 km from Duhai RRTS station.

## Approvals

The UGC approvals page lists the item "UGC notification - Deemed to be university" (the Gazette notification opens in an in-page viewer; the Ministry of Education notification is No. 9-16/2025-U.3(A), dated 6 November 2025, published in The Gazette of India, 22–28 November 2025). The AICTE approvals page lists AICTE approval letters from 1998-99 through 2026-27, including "AICTE APPROVAL LETTER 2026-27" and "AICTE APPROVAL LETTER 2025-26". The individual approval letters open in an in-page viewer; their contents were not retrievable as text.
