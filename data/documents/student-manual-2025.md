# Student Manual 2025

**Source:** https://www.kiet.edu/student-welfare/student-handbook/ (fetched 11 November 2026)
**Regime:** Institute-wide — student handbook.
**Fetched:** 11 November 2026

## What this page lists

The official Student Handbook hub lists four manuals: Student Manual 2025, Student Manual 2024, Student Manual 2023, and Student Manual 2022. Each opens as a PDF in an in-page modal viewer; no direct PDF URL is exposed, so the full text of the Student Manual 2025 could not be retrieved as text.

## What is known about the 2025 manual

The Student Manual 2025 is 160 pages. Its cover says Academic Year 2024-25 and it still uses "KIET Group of Institutions" branding (pre-university), despite the "2025" label on the hub. Its contents include orientation, attendance guidance, 26 years of KIET, courses and intake, and the Internship & Industry Partnership Cell (IIPC).

## Where the full text lives

The full manual text is only available through the modal viewer on the hub page above. A student or admin with browser access should open the hub page and read the Student Manual 2025 PDF there. This entry records only what the hub page itself states; it does not reproduce the manual's contents.
