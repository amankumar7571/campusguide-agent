# Fee Structure 2026-27

**Source:** "Fee Structure For 2026-27 Approved By Executive Council Of Krishna Institute Of Engineering & Technology — Deemed To Be University", official PDF at https://www.kiet.edu/assets/docs/admissions/UP SCHOLARSHIP DOCUMENTS KIET UNIVERSITY/2. NON REFUNDABLE ONLY FOR FIRST YEAR STUDENTS.pdf (fetched 11 November 2026); hostel fees from https://www.kiet.edu/admissions/fee-structure/ (fetched 11 November 2026)
**Regime:** Institute-wide — Fee Structure 2026-27. These are the 2026-27 rates for new admissions; the 2023–27 batch's fees at the time of its admission are not in this document.
**Fetched:** 11 November 2026

## Academic fee per annum (2026-27, non-refundable, first-year students)

B.Tech programmes (all specialisations): tuition fee Rs. 1,90,000 per annum, exam fee Rs. 8,500 per annum, total Rs. 1,98,500 per annum. This applies to B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, B.Tech in Computer Science, B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence), B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning), B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science), B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (Cyber Security), B.Tech in Computer Science & Information Technology, and B.Tech in Information Technology.

Other B.Tech programmes: B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering, B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering (VLSI Design & Technology), B.Tech in Electrical and Computer Engineering, B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, B.Tech in Advanced Mechatronics and Industrial Automation, and B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering: tuition fee Rs. 1,70,000 per annum, exam fee Rs. 8,500 per annum, total Rs. 1,78,500 per annum.

Other programmes: M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering and M.Tech in CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning): tuition fee Rs. 1,70,000, exam fee Rs. 10,000, total Rs. 1,80,000 per annum. Master in Computer Application (MCA): tuition fee Rs. 1,60,000, exam fee Rs. 8,500, total Rs. 1,68,500 per annum. Master in Business Administration (MBA): tuition fee Rs. 1,80,000, exam fee Rs. 8,500, total Rs. 1,88,500 per annum. D.Pharm: Rs. 95,000 tuition, Rs. 8,500 exam fee, total Rs. 1,03,500 per annum. B.Pharm: Rs. 1,70,000 tuition, Rs. 8,500 exam fee, total Rs. 1,78,500 per annum. M.Pharm (Pharmaceutics, Pharmacology, Pharmaceutical Quality Assurance): Rs. 1,70,000 tuition, Rs. 10,000 exam fee, total Rs. 1,80,000 per annum.

Caution money of Rs. 5,000 (refundable) is charged. The fee page for the current year did not state the academic year for the figures it shows; the figures above are from the 2026-27 fee structure document approved by the Executive Council.

## Hostel fee per year, including mess

Girls hostel: Single Occupancy Rs. 150,000 non-AC / Rs. 202,000 AC per year. Double Occupancy Rs. 145,000 non-AC / Rs. 187,000 AC per year. Triple / Four Occupancy Rs. 140,000 non-AC / Rs. 170,000 AC per year.

Boys hostel: Triple Occupancy Rs. 140,000 non-AC / Rs. 170,000 AC per year.

KIET has a campus hostel and also offers an off-campus hostel managed by a third party, in line with the standard of University Campus Hostels.

## Note on the lateral-entry fee

A separate lateral-entry fee structure for academic session 2026-27 lists a total academic fee of Rs. 1,52,999 (General) / Rs. 51,091 (FW). That figure differs from the per-annum figures above and was not reconciled with them; check the lateral-entry fee page (https://www.kiet.edu/admissions/lateral-fee-structure/) for lateral-entry students.
