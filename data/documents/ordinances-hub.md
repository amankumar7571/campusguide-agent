# Rules and Regulations Hub — University Ordinance

**Source:** https://www.kiet.edu/academics/ordinance/ (fetched 11 November 2026)
**Regime:** University Ordinance — new deemed-university batches. The University Ordinance does NOT govern the 2023–27 final-year batch (the "Affiliated Batch") on its face; that batch remains governed by AKTU rules.
**Fetched:** 11 November 2026

## What this page lists

The official Rules and Regulations hub lists four ordinances: Autonomous Ordinance, University Ordinance, Ph.D Ordinance, and Post Doc Ordinance. Each opens as a PDF in an in-page modal viewer; no direct PDF URL is exposed on the hub page, so the full text of the University Ordinance could not be retrieved as text.

## University Ordinance — what is known

The University Ordinance is titled "Academic Rules and Regulations Governing B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, MCA & M.Sc. Programs" (40 pages). It covers schools and departments, admission, credits, the teaching scheme, attendance (75% course-wise required for MSE and ESE, relaxation up to 15%, minimum 60% mandatory), grading (SGPA/CGPA), declaration of results, summer semester, re-admission, semester drop, and gold medal. Its version and date have not been established.

## Where the full text lives

The full University Ordinance text is only available through the modal viewer on the hub page above. A student or admin with browser access should open the hub page and read the University Ordinance PDF there. Do not treat the Autonomous Ordinance (a separate document in this system) as the University Ordinance — they are different documents under different regimes.
