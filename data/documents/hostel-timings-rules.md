# Hostel Timings and Entry-Out Rules

**Source:** https://www.old.kiet.edu/uploads/department/deansw/hostel/Hostel Timings and Entry-Out rules.pdf (full PDF text fetched 11 November 2026); facilities from https://www.kiet.edu/faq/ (fetched 11 November 2026)
**Regime:** Institute-wide — applies to all batches.
**Fetched:** 11 November 2026

## Girls' hostel — entry times

Normal entry time for all girls' hostel residents is 8:00 PM. This applies to all B.Tech / B.Pharm / MBA / MCA first-year students and to all B.Tech / B.Pharm 2nd, 3rd and 4th year, MCA 2nd and 3rd year, and MBA 2nd year students.

In an emergency, entry up to 9:00 PM is allowed with prior written permission of the rector/warden. Students attending coaching classes are not allowed after 8:00 PM (first-year students). For 2nd, 3rd and 4th year students attending coaching classes, entry up to 9:00 PM is allowed with a consent letter from parents.

No resident of a girls' hostel is allowed to go out of the institute campus after 7:30 PM at night or before 6:00 AM in the morning without a gate pass and sanctioned leave. All residents of girls' hostels must adhere to the timings strictly. Violation of these rules may lead to disciplinary action including expulsion from the hostel. If a student returns after the normal entry time, guards at the main gate should allow the student only after informing the concerned wardens.

## Boys' hostel — entry times

Normal entry time is 7:00 PM for Atithi Boys Hostel and Chandragupt Hall of Residence. Normal entry time is 10:00 PM for Tagore Hall of Residence, Aryabhatta Hall of Residence and Vivekanand Hall of Residence.

After 7:00 PM (for the 7:00 PM hostels) or after 9:30 PM (for the 10:00 PM hostels), a student returning late is allowed a maximum of 1 hour. If a student returns after 7:00 PM / 10:00 PM, the respective warden has to be informed first, and only then is entry of that student allowed. After 7:00 PM / 9:30 PM, if a student wants to stay out for more than 1 hour, they have to take a night out pass from the respective wardens. In case of emergency, residents have to make an entry in the register placed on the main gate and are allowed to stay out from the hostel only for 1 hour. Violation of these rules may lead to disciplinary action including expulsion from the hostel.

## Hostel facilities

KIET provides hostel facilities for both boys and girls. The hostels are furnished, Wi-Fi enabled, and supported by facilities such as study areas, recreation spaces, sports facilities, and security arrangements. Facilities mentioned include Wi-Fi/LAN connectivity, study halls, gyms, mess and dining facilities, indoor and outdoor sports, laundry, cafeteria, RO drinking water, hot/cold water, housekeeping, and security arrangements. Boys' hostels include C. V. Raman, Chandragupt, Vivekanand, Tagore, Aryabhatt, Chanakya, and Abhimanyu (off-campus). Girls' hostels have rectors/wardens with contacts listed on the hostel page (https://www.kiet.edu/campus-life/hostel/).
