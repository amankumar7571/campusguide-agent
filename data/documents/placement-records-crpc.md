# Placement Records and CRPC

**Source:** https://www.kiet.edu/placements/placement-recs/, https://www.kiet.edu/placements/team-crpc/ and https://www.kiet.edu/faq/ (fetched 11 November 2026)
**Regime:** Institute-wide — placement records and contacts. No official placement policy is included in this document: no official placement policy was found publicly on kiet.edu.
**Fetched:** 11 November 2026

## Placement records (2025 recruitment season)

- Record salary package achieved: Rs. 1.78 crore (the highest package achieved at KIET; it is the top individual offer and should not be interpreted as the salary received by every student).
- 370+ companies visited the campus for the 2025 recruitment season.
- 1,600+ students secured placements across diverse sectors.
- Students are trained in aptitude, verbal ability and soft skills.

## CSE department placement data

For CSE, the department page reports: 2024–25 — average package Rs. 5.95 LPA, highest package Rs. 24.23 LPA, placement percentage 90%. 2023–24 — average package Rs. 6.15 LPA, highest package Rs. 60 LPA, placement percentage 79.24%. These figures are specifically for CSE and should not be treated as the placement figures for every KIET programme. Placement outcomes vary considerably by branch, batch, student profile, skills and the roles offered by recruiters.

## CRPC — Corporate Relations & Placement Centre

KIET has a dedicated Corporate Relations & Placement Centre (CRPC) that handles industry interaction, internships and campus recruitment. Internship support is part of the placement ecosystem; the placement section includes a dedicated Internships@IIPC component, and academic departments highlight internships, live projects and industry collaborations.

## What is NOT in this document

There is no official placement policy, eligibility rulebook or "one student one offer" rule in this document, because no official placement policy was found publicly on the official KIET domain. A third-party copy of a 2026-batch placement policy exists on Scribd, but it is unverified and is for the 2026 batch, not the current final-year 2027 batch — it was deliberately NOT loaded. For current placement rules, contact CRPC directly.

## Note on placement statistics in this system

The placement-statistics table used by the SQL agent in this app still contains SAMPLE data (fictional companies such as SampleTech Solutions) — it is not real KIET placement data. The figures in this document are the real published figures; the SQL table is not.
