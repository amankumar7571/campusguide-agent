## B.Tech programmes

KIET Deemed to be University, Ghaziabad, offers B.Tech programmes across Computer Science, IT, Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical, Mechatronics and other emerging technology areas: Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), Computer Science, CSE (AI/AI&ML), Information Technology, Computer Science & Information Technology (CSIT), CSE (Data Science), CSE (Cyber Security), Electronics & Communication Engineering, Electrical & Electronics Engineering, Electrical & Computer Engineering, ECE (VLSI Design & Technology), Mechanical Engineering, and Advanced Mechatronics & Industrial Automation.

KIET offers B.Tech CSE (AI/AI&ML) programmes. The department's academic focus includes Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, NLP, Robotics and Agentic AI. KIET offers B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Cyber Security), focused on ethical hacking, network security, digital forensics, threat intelligence and cyber-risk management.

## Admission

For B.Tech admission, KIET lists 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM), with admission pathways through JEE Main, KIET Entrance Examination (KEE), and composite merit, as applicable to the programme and admission cycle. KEE stands for KIET Entrance Examination, conducted by KIET for admission to its flagship B.Tech programmes. Students who have completed or are appearing for Class XII with PCM can apply, subject to eligibility conditions. The final merit calculation and weightage are announced in the official admission information. Applicants should rely on the official admission portal and not on third-party consultants claiming to guarantee admission.

## Postgraduate and doctoral programmes

Postgraduate programmes include MBA, MCA, M.Tech CSE, M.Tech CSE (AI & ML), M.Pharm in Pharmacology, M.Pharm in Pharmaceutics, M.Pharm in Pharmaceutical Quality Assurance, and M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics & Computing. MCA is available with an intake of 300 students; eligibility includes graduation in any discipline, with or without Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level, with admission based on CUET(PG) score or qualifying-exam merit as applicable. Doctoral and post-doctoral programmes are also offered, including PhD areas such as Computer Science & Engineering, AI/ML, Data Science, Cyber Security, Engineering, Management, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, English and Pharmacy.

## Fees, scholarships, placements

The fee varies by programme. KIET's CSE programme page lists a tuition fee of Rs. 1.90 lakh per year. Full fee details are in the Fee Structure 2026-27 document in this system. KIET has a dedicated Scholarship Policy 2026 and provides scholarships based on applicable criteria, including merit-based scholarships at admission and for subsequent academic performance, sports scholarships, scholarships for programme/branch toppers, and academic performance incentives. Check the Scholarship Policy 2026 page for the latest eligibility conditions before applying.

Placement figures are in the Placement Records and CRPC document in this system. The record salary package is Rs. 1.78 crore; for the 2025 recruitment season, 370+ companies visited and 1,600+ students were placed.

## Centres of Excellence, clubs, sports and fests

KIET has a network of 14+ Centres of Excellence covering Artificial Intelligence, Supercomputing, Quantum Computing, Robotics, Space Technology, Cyber Security, Automotive Engineering, VLSI, E-Mobility and Industrial Automation. Key facilities include the Centre of Excellence for Supercomputing (NVIDIA DGX A100 infrastructure, supporting AI/ML project development, TensorFlow, PyTorch and CUDA), the Centre of Excellence for Quantum Computing, the Centre of Excellence for Industrial Automation by FESTO, an Apple Enabled Facility / Apple CoE, a Robotics & Mechatronics Centre, a Centre for Space Technologies, a Centre of Excellence for Power & Energy Systems (with Tata Power-DDL), a Centre of Excellence for Electric Mobility, a Power Semiconductor Devices CoE (with Mitsubishi Electric India), and a Research & Product Development Centre (with MBS India). KIET also has an AICTE IDEA Lab and an IPR policy supporting patent filings.

Student clubs include technical clubs (SAE, Dinobots, e-Yantra, Google DSC, Innogeeks Blockchain Club, KIET Design Club), cultural clubs (Creative Cell, KIET Dance Club, KIET Music Club, KIET Movie Society, SPIC MACAY, Odyssey, Quizzinga, Spark Creations), social initiatives (Uddeshhya – KIET Chapter), sports clubs (Dojo Karate Club, FC KIET), and initiatives such as KIET E-Cell, IEEE, Kavyanjali and Horizon College Magazine.

Sports: outdoor Cricket, Football, Basketball, Volleyball, Lawn Tennis, Kabaddi, Kho-Kho and Athletics; indoor Badminton, Table Tennis, Chess, Carrom and Billiards/Pool; plus E-Sports, Martial Arts, Shooting and Yoga. The annual RANN sports fest covers multiple disciplines. Cultural events: the major annual cultural fest is Epoque@Prastuti (music, dance, drama, fashion, literature, fine arts and digital media); other popular events include RANN, TEDxKIET, Innotech and Endeavour.
