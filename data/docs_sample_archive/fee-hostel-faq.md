# Fee and Hostel FAQ (Sample)

## Fee Payment
The semester fee must be paid before the start of each semester. A late fee of Rs. 500 per week is charged after the due date. Fee receipts are available on the student portal.

## Fee Refund
If a student withdraws admission before the start of the semester, the fee is refunded after deducting Rs. 5,000 as processing charges. No refund is given after classes begin.

## Hostel Rules
Hostel rooms are allotted on a first-come, first-served basis. The hostel in-time is 9:30 PM for all students. Visitors are allowed only between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM with prior permission from the warden.

## Mess
The mess serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Mess timings are 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM for breakfast, 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM for lunch, and 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM for dinner. Mess charges are included in the hostel fee.

## Scholarship
Merit scholarships are awarded to the top 5% of students in each branch based on CGPA. Students must apply for scholarships through the student portal before 30 September.
