# Code of Conduct (Sample)

## Identity Card
Every student must carry the college identity card on campus at all times and produce it when asked by faculty or security staff. A duplicate identity card costs Rs. 200.

## Ragging
Ragging is strictly prohibited and is a punishable offence. Any student found involved in ragging will face suspension and legal action as per university rules.

## Discipline
Students must maintain discipline in classrooms, laboratories, and the campus. Damage to college property will be charged to the responsible student.

## Dress Code
Students must wear the prescribed uniform on Mondays and during examinations, presentations, and placement drives. Formal attire is required for placement interviews.

## Grievance
Students may submit grievances to the Head of Department or through the grievance portal. Grievances are addressed within 15 working days.
