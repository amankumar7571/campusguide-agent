# Notice: End-Semester Examination Schedule (Sample)

## Examination Dates
The end-semester examinations for the odd semester will be held from 18 November to 28 November. The detailed date sheet is displayed on the notice board and the student portal.

## Reporting Time
Students must report to the examination hall 30 minutes before the start of the examination. The examination begins at 9:30 AM for the morning session and 1:30 PM for the afternoon session.

## Admit Card
Students must collect their admit cards from the department office by 15 November. Admit cards are issued only after fee clearance and attendance verification.

## Important Instructions
Students must carry their admit card and college identity card. Mobile phones, smart watches, and any electronic gadgets are strictly prohibited in the examination hall.
