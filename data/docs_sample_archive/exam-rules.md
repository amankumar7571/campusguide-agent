# Examination Rules (Sample)

## Mid-Semester Examination
The Mid-Semester Examination (MSE) carries 30 marks per theory subject. Two MSEs may be held and the best score is counted. MSE dates are announced at least two weeks in advance.

## End-Semester Examination
The end-semester examination carries 70 marks per theory subject. Practical examinations carry 50 marks. A student must score at least 40% overall in a subject to pass.

## Unfair Means
Use of unfair means, including copying, carrying unauthorised material, or impersonation, leads to cancellation of the examination and disciplinary action. Mobile phones are not allowed inside the examination hall.

## Re-evaluation
A student may apply for re-evaluation of an answer script within 10 days of the result declaration by paying the prescribed fee. Re-evaluation results are final.

## Admit Card
The admit card is issued only after fee clearance and attendance verification. Students must carry the admit card and college identity card to every examination.
