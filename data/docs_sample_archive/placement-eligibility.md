# Placement Eligibility Rules (Sample)

## Eligibility Criteria
A student is eligible for campus placements if they have a CGPA of 6.0 or above, no active backlogs at the time of the drive, and at least 75% attendance in the final year.

## Backlog Policy
Students with one backlog may appear for drives only with written permission from the Training and Placement Cell. Students with two or more active backlogs are not eligible until the backlogs are cleared.

## Registration
Placement registration is mandatory and must be completed on the placement portal before the deadline announced by the Training and Placement Cell. Late registration is not accepted.

## Dream Company Rule
A student who has already received one offer may apply only to dream companies, defined as companies offering a CTC of 8 LPA or above, or a role in a core domain of the student's branch.

## Offer Policy
A student may hold a maximum of two offers. After accepting a second offer, the student must inform the Training and Placement Cell within three days.
