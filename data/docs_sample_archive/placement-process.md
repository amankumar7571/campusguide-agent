# Placement Process (Sample)

## Placement Drives
Companies visit the campus for placement drives from August to April. Each drive includes a pre-placement talk, an aptitude or coding test, technical interviews, and an HR interview.

## Pre-Placement Talk
The pre-placement talk explains the company profile, job role, CTC, and work location. Attendance at the pre-placement talk is mandatory for registered students.

## Selection Process
Shortlisted students appear for technical interviews followed by an HR interview. Results are announced by the Training and Placement Cell, and selected students receive offer letters through the placement portal.

## Internship to PPO
Students selected for internships of six months or more may receive a Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) based on performance. The PPO is confirmed by the company before the final semester.

## Placement Support
The Training and Placement Cell conducts mock interviews, resume reviews, and aptitude training every semester. Students should keep their resume updated on the placement portal.
