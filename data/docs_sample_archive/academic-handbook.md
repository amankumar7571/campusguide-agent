# Academic Handbook (Sample Excerpt)

## Programme Structure
The B.Tech programme runs for four years, divided into eight semesters. Each semester has 15 weeks of teaching followed by examinations. A student must earn 160 credits to graduate.

## Credits and Grading
Each theory course carries 3 to 4 credits and each laboratory course carries 1 to 2 credits. Grading follows a 10-point scale. A CGPA of 7.5 or above is considered first division with distinction.

## Attendance Requirement
A student must maintain at least 75% attendance in every subject to be eligible to appear in the end-semester examination. Attendance is counted from the first day of the semester.

## Leave Rules
Medical leave must be supported by a certificate from a registered medical practitioner and submitted within three working days of rejoining. Leave does not reduce the 75% attendance requirement except through the condonation process.

## Academic Calendar
The odd semester runs from July to December and the even semester runs from January to June. The academic calendar is published on the notice board at the start of each semester.
