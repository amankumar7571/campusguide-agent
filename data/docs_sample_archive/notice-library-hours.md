# Notice: Library Timings (Sample)

## Library Hours
The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays (Monday to Friday) and from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on Saturdays. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Book Issue Rules
A student may issue up to 3 books at a time for 14 days. Books can be renewed once if no other student has reserved them. A fine of Rs. 2 per day is charged for late return.

## Reading Room
The reading room is open during library hours and has seating for 200 students. Silence must be maintained in the reading room at all times.

## Digital Library
The digital library provides access to e-journals and e-books from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays. Students must log in with their college email ID.
