# Attendance Rules (Sample)

## Minimum Attendance
Every student must secure a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject, separately for theory and laboratory classes. Attendance below 75% in any subject makes the student detained in that subject.

## Condonation
Condonation of shortage of attendance up to 10% may be granted by the Head of Department on genuine medical grounds. This means a student with 65% to 75% attendance may be condoned if medical certificates are verified. Attendance below 65% cannot be condoned under any circumstances.

## Detained List
The detained list is finalised one week before the end-semester examination. A detained student must repeat the subject in the next available semester and cannot appear for that subject's examination.

## Attendance Monitoring
Attendance is recorded daily and updated on the student portal every week. Students can check their attendance on the portal and must report any discrepancy to the class coordinator within three days.
